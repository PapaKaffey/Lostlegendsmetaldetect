I’m Alex Kaffenberger.

I’m a dad from Plattsmouth, a local history buff, and the person who answers when you text Lost Legends.

Text Alex Request a search
For the fastest reply, text the item, an address or GPS pin, and a photo of the search area.

Why I do it

I got into metal detecting through old maps and local history. Helping someone get their wedding ring back gave me another reason to go out with the detector. I help people recover items that carry a story, whether they’re wedding bands, family jewelry, or the keys they need to finish their day.

What a search looks like

We start where you last remember having the item. I adjust my detector for the local soil and work in overlapping passes. Along I-29, bits of aluminum made a lost ring hard to hear. Checking the signal from a second direction helped me pick it out.

On your property

I pinpoint the target before I dig. In a lawn, I cut a clean plug, refill the hole neatly, and replace the turf whenever conditions allow. I ask before sharing photos or details that identify you or your property.
