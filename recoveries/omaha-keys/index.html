Key recovery

Keys beneath the fresh mulch

A set of keys went missing during a pipe installation in Omaha. By lunchtime, the owner had a freshly mulched work area to search.

Omaha, NebraskaPublished April 10, 2026
By Alex Kaffenberger

When he called me, he’d already spent more than two hours looking. We narrowed the most likely stretch to about twenty feet.

The keys and the other metal on the keyring produced a choppy signal under the mulch. I slowed down and checked the uneven sounds as well as the clear ones.

About 45 minutes later, I pulled out the missing keys. The owner could finally put them back in his pocket and get on with his day.
