Snow recovery

A wedding band lost in the snow

A family got in touch after a Celtic wedding band disappeared in the snow. Finding it meant putting their wedding set back together.

Omaha area
By Alex Kaffenberger

I went out to search the area where they remembered losing it. The snow had hidden the band from view. It took less than an hour to bring it back.

The family shared the photo with their testimonial after the search. Their words sum up what the recovery meant to them: “He was able to find our ring and reunite the set!”
