Reward basis

Lost ring & jewelry recovery

I search yards, fields, snow, and shallow water for lost rings, jewelry, keys, and other metal keepsakes. I’m based in Plattsmouth and take searches on both sides of the Missouri.

Text Alex Request a search
For the fastest reply, text the item, an address or GPS pin, and a photo of the search area.

How the reward works

You set the reward after I return your item. No find, no fee. The only exception is a travel fee we agree on before a remote trip.

If I don’t find it

You’ll get a report of where I searched and practical next steps, such as another spot to check or whether a return visit makes sense. You pay nothing unless we agreed on a travel fee for a remote trip in advance.

Water, snow, and other tough searches

For a ring lost while swimming or wading, mark where you entered the water and where you noticed it was missing. Send me the depth and bottom conditions so I can judge access and equipment. For a snow loss, tell me where the snow was piled and when the area was last plowed or shoveled.

Before I arrive

Mark where you last had the item and note any ground or snow that has been moved. If someone else manages the property, we’ll arrange permission before the search.

Searching your yard

I pinpoint the target before I dig. In a lawn, I cut a clean plug, refill the hole neatly, and replace the turf whenever conditions allow. I ask before sharing photos or details that identify you or your property.
